Virtual Laboratory Procedure

  1. Select TCSC, SSSC or UPFC.
  2. Set V₁, V₂, XL and transmission power angle δ.
  3. For TCSC, vary the compensation factor kc.
  4. For SSSC, vary the injected series voltage.
  5. For UPFC, vary Vse, θse and Vsh.
  6. Observe the changing phasor relationships.
  7. Observe P, Q, current and effective reactance.
  8. Compare the P−δ characteristics of the three FACTS controllers.
